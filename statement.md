
Problem Statement

Managing student, course, enrollment, and academic records manually can be time-consuming and may lead to errors or duplicate information. There is a need for a simple and organized system that can store and manage academic records efficiently.

The Campus Course & Records Manager (CCRM) is developed as a Java-based application to simplify the management of student and course-related information. It provides users with functionalities to manage records, enroll students in courses, and maintain academic information through a structured system.

Scope of the Project

The scope of the CCRM project includes:

Managing student information and academic records.
Managing course details.
Enrolling students in available courses.
Managing student grades and enrollment records.
Searching and filtering student/course information.
Importing and exporting data using files such as CSV.
Providing input validation and error handling.
Demonstrating Java concepts such as OOP, collections, exception handling, file handling, and Stream API.
Providing a foundation that can later be extended into a database or web-based application.
Target Users

The primary target users are:

College/University Administrators – to manage student and course records.
Faculty/Instructors – to manage course and student academic information.
Students – to view or manage relevant academic and enrollment information.
Academic Staff – to maintain organized academic records.
High-Level Features
Student Management – Add, view, update, search, and manage student records.
Course Management – Maintain course information, credits, and availability.
Enrollment Management – Enroll students into courses and maintain enrollment records.
Grade Management – Record and update student grades.
Search & Filtering – Quickly find required student or course records.
Data Import/Export – Read and write academic information using CSV/file-based storage.
Validation & Exception Handling – Handle invalid inputs and prevent incorrect operations.
Academic Records – Maintain organized information related to student courses and performance.
Backup/Data Handling – Support safe management of stored records.
